What are all these files?
  • Audio — each person's untouched lossless 24-bit WAV, all lined up to start together.
  • Normalised — the same, turned up/down to −16 LUFS (podcasts) or −23 LUFS (EBU R128 broadcast).
  • Room tone — the quiet section, for noise reduction.
  • Rough mix — everyone together with your intro and outro, at −16 LUFS. Handy for a quick listen.
  • Video — each camera as an MP4, lined up the same way. "Camera original" is the untouched file.
  • Session — the .sesx opens straight in Audition (beta); the .xml opens in Premiere, Resolve and Audition. Everything on its own track, with markers. Keep them in the same folder as the audio.
  • Transcript — who said what: .txt to read, .srt / .vtt subtitles for Premiere or YouTube.
  • Chapters — from your markers: .json for podcast apps, and a YouTube description list.
