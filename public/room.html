Good to know

  • Use Chrome, Edge or Safari on a laptop or desktop. Plug in to power if you can.
  • On a phone? That's fine — plug in headphones, put it on charge, turn on Do Not Disturb, and keep this page open on screen the whole time (switching apps or locking the screen can pause your microphone).
  • Your voice is recorded on your computer at full quality and saved to your disk as you go — a bad connection or even a crash won't lose it.
  • Keep this tab open until it says Saved after recording stops. If it closes early, just open the same link again and it carries on uploading.
