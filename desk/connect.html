🎛 Rolling Desk

Your producer's desk. Mix the show, run it, and keep every recording.

On your Rolling dashboard, copy the link under Open studio as host for your show.

A live full-quality copy is saved while you record, then every finished file when the take is ready. About 4 GB per person per hour while recording.

No server needed: Rolling Desk runs the whole studio on this computer and makes an internet link for your guests. Everything works as on a server — the Console, recordings, transcripts, all the files.

About 4 GB per person per hour of recording. Use a drive with plenty of space.

  1. No Cloudflare account yet? Sign up free, then add your domain (or buy one there).
  2. At one.dash.cloudflare.com go to Networks › Tunnels › Create a tunnel › Cloudflared, name it (e.g. rolling).
  3. Copy the install command it shows and paste it below (or just the long token at its end). You don't need to run it.
  4. Press Next and add a Public hostname: your address, type HTTP, URL localhost:8790. Save.

Kept only on this computer, encrypted.

Advanced
  • This computer is the studio for the show: keep it on, awake and online until the files are ready (Rolling Desk stops it sleeping).
  • Everyone's full-quality recording comes to this computer: a home broadband connection is fine.
  • If the internet drops, the call drops but no recording is lost: they upload when people come back.
